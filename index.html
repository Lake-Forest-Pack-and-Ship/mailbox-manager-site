Mailbox Manager

A desktop app for keeping track of rented mailboxes: who rents each box, when it's due for renewal, and what's been paid.

What it does

  • Keeps a record of each mailbox and its holder, including business names and forwarding addresses.
  • Shows which boxes are coming up for renewal and records renewals and payments.
  • Keeps an inventory of boxes, their sizes, and their prices.
  • Backs up its data every day on the computer it runs on.

Backing up to Google Drive

Mailbox Manager can also keep a copy of each daily backup in Google Drive, so the records are safe even if the computer is lost or broken. This is optional and is turned on in the app's Settings with Connect Google Drive.

When connected, the app saves its backups to a folder called Mailbox Manager Backups in the Google Drive you sign in with, and deletes old backups there to save space. It asks Google only for access to the files it creates itself, so it can't see or change anything else in your Google Drive.

Read the privacy policy for exactly what the app does with your Google account.

Mailbox Manager is made for use at Lake Forest Pack and Ship and isn't offered to the public. The app runs entirely on your own computer; there's no Mailbox Manager website or account to sign up for.
